## What ColIA is

The name joins two parts: **Col**, from Colombia, and **IA**, the Spanish initials for artificial intelligence.

It's a lab because we run experiments, measure and publish what comes out, including what fails. And it's a community because nobody learns alone: what one person figures out saves everyone else part of the way.

## Why ColIA

Lately, many communities have grown around AI, across all the fields it touches. But in Colombia there wasn't a place to learn and grow in AI, either professionally or as a community.

That's where ColIA comes from: an open, collaborative community that brings together people willing to build and learn in the open, to give back as a community, and to welcome anyone who wants to be part of this change.

ColIA is a place to show the talent and creativity of its members. We're not after a profit, but a space to learn and grow together.

## How we work

Everything published at ColIA follows six principles:

1. **Open.** Code, models and recipes (settings for running models, measured on real hardware) are published under open licenses. When we start from another company's model, we say so and publish under the most open license it allows.
2. **Measured.** Every claim comes with numbers, measured on real hardware: from a laptop without a graphics card to a workstation with several.
3. **Bilingual.** Everything ships in English and Spanish. Spanish is our language: we write it natively, not as a translation.
4. **Local.** What we make runs on your computer, without depending on a cloud, and your data never leaves it.
5. **Community.** Anyone who wants to learn, explore or contribute is welcome, whatever they know.
6. **Places.** Our models are named after places in Colombia that deserve to be better known: not the big cities, but places that are sometimes forgotten and are also beautiful, diverse and full of talent.

## What we're starting with

We're preparing two things:

- **A model**: a small one that runs on your own computer and is named after a place in Colombia. It's coming soon.
- **An app** to use AI models on your own computer, with settings measured on real hardware. It's in development.

We'll introduce them here on the blog over the next few days. If you want to be the first to know, join our [Discord](https://colia.org/discord).

## Join us

You don't need to know everything. ColIA starts with one person learning in the open, and one day it will be a community by everyone and for everyone. If you want to learn, find people to build with, or make small things that help, there's room for you.

<aside class="nota">ColIA isn't a company, but a collaborative project in progress. Anyone who joins does so as a volunteer, not under a contract.</aside>

- **Chat with us** on [Discord](https://colia.org/discord): meet people, ask questions and organize projects. Or in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/colia-labs/discussions): questions, ideas or whatever you're building.
- **Contribute** by following the [guide](https://github.com/colia-labs/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). It's not all code: asking questions, measuring on your computer or improving a text helps too.
- **Read the [code of conduct](https://github.com/colia-labs/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)**: it's short, and it helps us keep the community welcoming and productive.
- **Follow us** on [X](https://x.com/colialabs), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/colialabs) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/colia-labs).
