How we work

  1. Open. Code, models and recipes are published under open licenses.
  2. Measured. Every claim comes with numbers, measured on modest hardware.
  3. Bilingual. Everything ships in English and Spanish. The Spanish is native, not a translation.
  4. Local. What we make runs on your computer, without depending on a cloud.
  5. Community. Anyone who wants to learn, explore or contribute is welcome, whatever they know.

Join us
